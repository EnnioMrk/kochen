# Rezeptor — product truth

A private German recipe PWA for one family and their friends. Members capture recipes
from photos, links, video or text; cook them step by step with timers; keep variations;
rate what they cooked; and build a shopping list from several recipes. There is no
public page: only approved `ennio.vip` accounts reach the app.

## Who it is for

A household of mixed cooking skill, on a phone propped against a kitchen wall, and
sometimes on a laptop at the table. They share recipes with friends by QR invite. They
read German. They expect a cookbook, not a growth product.

## Jobs

| Job | Success looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Capture | A recipe from a photo, link, video or voice note becomes a clean draft the member confirms. |
| Cook | One step at display size, a timer that survives a reload, the next step within thumb reach. |
| Rate | A cook event exists before a rating, so the family's average means something. |
| Vary | Fork a recipe, change a line, compare side by side, and keep the lineage. |
| Shop | Several recipes and servings become one list with merged units. |
| Share | A friend opens a private link, reads and comments, and cannot see actions that would fail. |
| Find | One natural-language field becomes a strict filter, not a keyword guess. |

## Constraints

- German only. No public page, `noindex` on every response, `robots.txt` disallow.
- Phone first, offline-tolerant, installable. Print is a first-class output (A4).
- Family data stays inside the app. Private links require a login and are read-only.
- The palette must survive a bright kitchen, a dark evening and a printed sheet.
- Every member may personalise the look; the account carries the choice to other devices.

## Surfaces

Recipe list, recipe detail, cook mode, capture and import review, editor, shopping,
friends and profiles, search, settings, admin, install, print.

## Voice

Plain German, short sentences, the rule stated instead of hidden. "Gekocht",
"6-mal gekocht · zuletzt", "Löschen blendet das Rezept aus. Varianten bleiben sichtbar."
